# Human-sounding text

The rules are in `references/human-copy-rules.md`. Sections A (signs of AI), K (any genre) and the checklist are for any text; the sections on platforms and replies are for social networks.

Order:
1. Before a draft take 3-5 samples of the same genre from the corpus (item 36); write the draft yourself, from the task's inputs; the headline and the first line get 3-5 variants with different moves (item 40).
2. Go through section A and the checklist; a score below 35 of 50 means rewrite.
3. The project rules and the owner's words outrank this file: an owner's decision that argues with a rule means you fix the rule, you do not keep both side by side.
